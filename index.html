Starfall Meadow

Ages ~7–10 · 60 seconds a round

Stars arc across the sky and Pip runs to catch them. You can't win by chasing where a star is — you have to read its curve and get to where it's going to be. Missed stars bloom into flowers, so there's no losing.

Starbounce

Ages ~7–10 · 90 seconds a round

Zip's Sky Rescue. Aim a cannon, set the power, and launch Zip in an arc to pop the golden stars and free the friends inside. Bank off the candy walls and jelly blobs — and the sparkle guide gets shorter the better you predict.

Beacon Bridge

Ages ~7–10 · 90 seconds a crossing

A fox has to cross a canyon before the light goes. Each of the three stone spans has a bite out of it, and it takes two blocks to make one whole — a lower one that slots into the notch, then an upper one that caps it off. Turn both in your head before you drop either.

Mental rotation Two-step planning 2D · works offline

Foxglove Trail

Ages ~7–10 · 3 nights, under 2 minutes

A fox must reach the den before the fog closes in. You only see a few paces of wood — but a paper map shows the whole forest, always drawn the same way up as the screen. Match the landmarks, pick the right branch, and keep your line home.
