Four browser games for kids. No install, no accounts — just open and play.

Starfall Meadow

Ages ~7–10 · 60 seconds a round

Stars arc across the sky and Pip runs to catch them. You can't win by chasing where a star is — you have to read its curve and get to where it's going to be. Missed stars bloom into flowers, so there's no losing.

Comet Catch

Ages ~6–10 · 75 seconds a round

Bloop's Sky Carnival, in 3D. Flower cannons fire comets in long readable arcs and you run to where each one will land, basket ready. Dodge the sneezy clouds and watch out for berries that change course mid‑flight.

Starbounce

Ages ~7–10 · under 2 minutes a level

Zip's Sky Rescue. Aim a cannon, set the power, and launch Zip in an arc to pop the golden stars and free the friends inside. Bank off the candy walls and jelly blobs — and the sparkle guide gets shorter the better you predict.

Beacon Bridge

Ages ~7–10 · about a minute a crossing

A fox needs to cross a canyon, and every stone span has a piece missing. Turn the floating block in your head, slide it over the gap, and drop it in. Five clean spans and the beacon on the far cliff lights up.
